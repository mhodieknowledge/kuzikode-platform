## Direction contract

THESIS: Kuzikode as the tenant directory of a well-run modernist office building: everything numbered, grooved, in its place, one lit indicator showing where you are. Refuses the dark-hero + stock team photo + grid of identical icon cards default.

OWN-WORLD: deep felt navy (#0B1428) boards with white condensed-caps directory rows on groove lines; concrete-light (#F3F5F8) reading grounds; cobalt (#2F57FF / #1F44E0) spent only on the lit, active, primary thing. Archivo across widths (condensed caps for directory, wide display), JetBrains Mono for every figure. Round arrow knobs, solid inverted active states, 10 px cards, pills.

STORY: the visitor sees what Kuzikode does in six numbered floors, sees real "from" prices, opens the floor that fits, compares packages without leaving the phone, and starts a project with the package preselected.

FIRST VIEWPORT: phone: felt board full-bleed; display headline (2–3 lines) left; one sentence; a "What do you need?" directory of 4 routes as tappable rows (website · app or system · AI or automation · pay an invoice) that is the working action; primary "Start a project" pill pinned in the header. Desktop: headline left 7 cols, live directory board right 5 cols with the lit row cross-fading a real product UI frame.
