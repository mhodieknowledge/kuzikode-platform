## Direction contract

THESIS: Kuzikode as the road sign at the junction: plain words, a number for every route, the starting price on every plate, one green you trust because it never changes. Refuses the dark-hero + bright-CTA + stock-team + identical-icon-cards default and the near-black + neon AI look.

OWN-WORLD: sign green (#0B4A2D) owns the first viewport, the recommended package and the footer; verge (#F1F4F0) and paper carry reading; mint (#7FE3A8) is spent only on lit things on green. Overpass (Highway Gothic descendant) for all words, Overpass Mono for every figure. Plates: 7 px corners with an inset double ring; route shields; a road strip with stop plates. No pills, no round arrow knobs, no eyebrows.

STORY: the visitor reads one plain headline, sees six numbered route plates each with its real starting price, takes the plate that fits, compares that division's packages side by side (one column at a time on a phone), and requests a package with it preselected on the contact form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: phone: sign-green field; headline (3–4 lines) left; one sentence; the stack of six route plates is the working choice; "Get a quote" plate button in the header from 520 px, in the menu sheet below. Desktop: headline left 6 cols, the gantry of six plates (2 × 3) right 6 cols.
