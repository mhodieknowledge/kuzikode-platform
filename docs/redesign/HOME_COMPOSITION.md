# Home composition (R10.2)

## Content the home must carry
Positioning (one line + tag) · the 5 featured divisions (name, one-line value, **"from" price only**, link) · why Kuzikode (real promises only, CMS `features`) · how we work (CMS `process`, 4 steps) · pricing entry point (/pricing) · selected work and client words (only when published/consented, as today) · contact: Get a quote, WhatsApp, phone, email (Settings-backed, `nav.footer.contact`).
No stats, client logos, ratings or invented claims. Services use **section icons** (the site's single Lucide icon family, one hue per division), never the division SVG illustrations.

## Sections (mobile 390 first, then 1440), with source images
1. **Hero**: full-bleed photo (HOME_IMAGES.hero) with dark overlay; content anchored at the bottom: tag pill, H1, one-line sub, primary "Get a quote" pill with circular arrow (5052) + WhatsApp ghost pill; a row of 5 frosted division chips with their section icons (5017); two glass widgets on the photo: price tile "Websites from $X" with arrow (5063, 5014) and a WhatsApp reply tile (5050). Desktop: widgets float right.
2. **Marquee band** (5067): the 5 division names separated by a glyph, CSS-only, static under reduced motion; docks under the hero.
3. **Services** (5051 peeking track, 5064 corner icon circle, 5059 notched price tab, 5013 outlined rows): each division a card: corner circle with its section icon in the division hue, name, one-line value, notched "From $X" tab, arrow link. Phones: swipe track with the next card peeking + dots, "See all services" button below (5062). Desktop: 5-up row (5065) or wide-first bento.
4. **Why** (5049 2x2 icon grid with hairlines, 5058 "included in all" idea): CMS `features`, section icons, hairline dividers, no numbers.
5. **How** (5066 numbered steps joined by a dashed line, 5015 stepper): vertical on phones, horizontal on desktop; CMS `process`.
6. **Pricing entry** (5072 dark band, 5057 CTA card, 5061 pill CTA): one dark rounded band, "See every package" with the five "from" prices as compact rows (5013) and a pill to /pricing.
7. **Work / client words**: only when published (existing data rules), rebuilt in the new style.
8. **Closing band** (5016 overlap, 5065 dark photo CTA, 5071 reassurance line): photo (HOME_IMAGES.closing) with overlay, one line, Get a quote + WhatsApp, three contact rows.

Skipped: logo marquees, stat strips, testimonial walls, comparison matrix (5071), toggles, 3D gloss.
