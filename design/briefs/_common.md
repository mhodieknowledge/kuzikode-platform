# Shared rules for every Kuzikode mockup
- Company: Kuzikode Technologies (Zimbabwe). Positioning: "Building intelligent digital solutions."
- Use ONLY the copy in the brief. Do not invent client names, logos, testimonials, statistics, awards, team members or photos of people.
- No stock photos of people. No robots, circuit boards, neon, rainbow gradients. At most one subtle grid texture or radial glow in a dark hero.
- Tokens: ink #0A0F1C / #1C2333 / #475066, muted #8A93A6, line #E4E7EE, surface #FFFFFF, canvas #F7F8FA, brand blue #2747E8 (hover #3355FF, tint #EEF1FF), accent amber #E8A33D (max once per view), success #11845B, warning #B25E09, danger #C2362F.
- Type: Manrope (headings, 600-800, tight tracking), Inter (body/UI), JetBrains Mono for references like INV-2026-00012. Tabular numbers for money.
- Radius 6 (inputs/buttons), 10 (cards), 16 (feature panels). Borders over shadows. 4px spacing grid.
- Logo: lowercase "kuzikode" wordmark with a K mark made of a dark vertical stem and two offset slanted arms (upper arm blue).
